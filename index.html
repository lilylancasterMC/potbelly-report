Where Potbelly stands with consumers today

Five reads from Morning Consult's Daily Tracker: the audience that's indifferent to Potbelly and where they go instead, a broader look at who Potbelly's fans already are — including awareness by geography and appetite for a higher-protein menu — how Potbelly compares to the sandwich category, a forward-looking read on the value opportunity specifically, and two partnership angles worth exploring.

Who is the indifferent audience, and where do they go instead?

"Indifferent" means aware of Potbelly but registering no opinion — distinct from never having heard of it, and distinct from disliking it. Nationally, that group is nearly as large as everyone who's favorable.

Where U.S. adults sit on Potbelly

Among adults who know the brand (the ~45% who aren't "never heard of"), indifference is the largest single camp — 19.9 points of that 44.7-point aware population are neutral, versus 18.8 favorable and just 6.1 unfavorable. Roughly four in nine people who know Potbelly haven't formed an opinion either way — a relevance problem to solve, not a reputation problem to fix.

Health, wellness, and appetite for protein

Protein bars are a mainstream habit

22.6% of U.S. adults regularly eat protein bars or meal-replacement bars, and another 42.7% do so sometimes — only 34.8% never do.

+15.8 vs +9.2 net fav.

Health-conscious consumers already favor Potbelly more

Adults who say health is a primary factor in their food choices give Potbelly higher favorability (+15.8) and net-positive purchase consideration (+3.4) versus less health-focused adults (+9.2 / −3.4).

Sample-size note: a direct cut of protein habits among adults already favorable toward Potbelly returned only 12 weighted respondents — too small to cite. Directionally it points the same way (76% call high protein important, 56% regularly eat protein bars, both above the general-population rate), but this needs a larger, purpose-built sample before it's used to size an opportunity.

Value perception moves sharply with household finances

+20.3 vs +8.2 net value

Financially strained households value Potbelly far less

Adults whose finances improved over the past year rate Potbelly's value at +20.3 net; adults whose finances worsened — the larger group — rate it at +8.2.

+9.3 vs −1.8 consideration

And they're less likely to buy

Purchase consideration flips from net-positive among financially improving adults (+9.3) to net-negative among financially strained adults (−1.8).

With value already the largest gap versus the competitive set, Potbelly's growth math leans on a consumer segment that is actively pulling back — reinforcing why value messaging and loyalty economics matter more here than for the rest of the category.

Source: Morning Consult Daily Tracker, U.S. adults.

Where the value message should concentrate

+20.3 vs +8.2 net value

Financially strained households are the most sensitive, and the most addressable

Adults whose finances improved over the past year rate Potbelly's value at +20.3; adults whose finances worsened rate it at +8.2. That's a specific, targetable segment — not a diffuse brand-wide problem.

2

Two concrete levers, both already in Potbelly's 2026 plan

Loyalty/app usage and catering are the levers most likely to convert the trial that's already driving the fan-lift above into a durable value perception, rather than competing on price alone.

The forward-looking read: Potbelly's value problem behaves like an awareness problem wearing a value label. The brand doesn't score lower among people who've tried it — it scores dramatically higher, and by more than the category leaders gain from their own fans. The lever most likely to move the topline number isn't a deeper discount; it's getting more people through the door — via loyalty enrollment, catering trial, and value-forward promotion concentrated in the newer, lower-awareness 2026 markets — and letting the same conversion that already happens in the Midwest play out everywhere else.

Source: Morning Consult Daily Tracker, U.S. adults.

Partnership opportunities, based on audience overlap

Two angles that fall out of comparing Potbelly's own audience to other brand audiences: a wellness/GLP-1 adjacency its customer base already leans into, and a set of cross-category brand affinities — retail, beverages, fitness tracking, and apparel — where Potbelly's fans already skew favorable.

Potbelly's base already over-indexes on GLP-1s

16.3% of Potbelly customers are currently on a GLP-1 medication vs. 8.3% of everyone else — and 27.9% have GLP-1 experience overall vs. 13.3%.

+23.0 vs +8.8 net fav.

It runs both directions

Adults currently on a GLP-1 medication also rate Potbelly's favorability (+23.0) and purchase consideration (+10.1 net) well above non-users (+8.8 / −0.3 net).

Read together, this looks less like a future risk to manage and more like an audience Potbelly already over-indexes with — a plausible fit for a wellness, nutrition-tracking, or GLP-1-adjacent brand partnership, or for menu messaging built around protein and portion flexibility.
